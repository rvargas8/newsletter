Math & Science

Math

  • This week, we will review for our Topic 7 Assessment. Students will practice adding multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm, regrouping, and solving real-world word problems.

Science

  • In Topic 2, Lesson 2: Changes in Energy, we will continue exploring kinetic energy, potential energy, and work. Students will investigate how energy can cause objects to move or change.

Reading, Writing & Social Studies

Reading

  • In Module 4, Week 2, we will read The Traveling Trio and Gigi and the Wishing Ring. Students will practice summarizing, retelling important events, identifying elements of drama, and determining the theme of a story. Our vocabulary strategy will continue focusing on shades of meaning.

Writing

  • In Module 4, Week 2, we will continue practicing how to identify and correct sentence fragments and run-on sentences. Students will work on writing complete sentences and expressing their ideas clearly. We will also practice writing the cursive letters m and n.

Grammar

  • In Module 4, Week 2, we will learn how to form plural nouns, including changing y to i and adding -es, as well as identifying and using irregular plural nouns.

Social Studies

  • In Chapter 3, Lesson 1: America’s First Peoples, we will learn about early Native American communities and explore how geography and natural resources influenced where people settled and how they lived.
