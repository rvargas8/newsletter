Weekly Update, Families!

Hello Barracuda Families! The third week of school is in motion, and we are off to a great start! Please make sure your child brings their red folder to school every day, as many students have been forgetting them.

Math & Science

Math

  • Compare and order numbers up to 10,000
  • Use place value and number lines to round numbers to the nearest 10 and 100

Science

  • Explore the properties of matter
  • Compare materials and objects based on properties such as mass, hardness, and texture

Reading, Writing & Social Studies

Reading

  • Monitor and clarify our understanding
  • Explore figurative language and literary elements
  • Use context clues to determine the meaning of unfamiliar words

Writing

  • Continue writing personal narratives
  • Practice the cursive letters d and g

Grammar

  • Learn about and practice identifying the different kinds of sentences

Social Studies

  • Learn about the characteristics of rural, suburban, and urban communities and what makes each type of community unique
