Math & Science

Math

  • In Topic 7, Lessons 5–8, we will practice adding multi-digit whole numbers using the standard algorithm, including regrouping when needed. We will also apply our addition strategies to solve real-world comparison word problems.

Science

  • In Topic 2, Lesson 2: Changes in Energy, we will explore how energy causes motion and change. Students will learn about kinetic energy, potential energy, and work and investigate how energy makes everyday objects move.

Reading, Writing & Social Studies

Reading

  • In Module 4, Week 1, we will read The Saga of Pecos Bill and focus on identifying literary elements, elements of drama, and figurative language. Our vocabulary strategy will focus on shades of meaning, exploring how words with similar meanings can express different levels of intensity.

Writing

  • In Module 4, Week 1, we will learn how to identify and correct sentence fragments and run-on sentences. Students will practice writing complete sentences and combining or separating ideas correctly. We will also practice writing the cursive letters r and s.

Grammar

  • In Module 4, Week 1, we will learn about subject and object pronouns and practice choosing pronouns that agree with the nouns they replace.

Social Studies

  • We will review the concepts covered in Chapter 2 and prepare for our Chapter 2 Assessment. Students will revisit how geography, natural resources, and human activities affect the environment and the way people live.
