Weekly Update, Families!

Hello Barracuda Families! We have more exciting events coming up as October continues! Our Trunk or Treat and Haunted Hallway event is happening on Saturday, October 24th. It’s sure to be a fun time for our Barracuda families!

No signature is needed—it’s simply a quick way for us to stay connected about classroom behavior.

Thank you for your continued support!

Math & Science

Math

  • This week, we will review and prepare for our Topic 14 Assessment, strengthening our understanding of multiplying greater numbers using place value, multiplication patterns, arrays, and the distributive property.

Science

  • In Topic 2, Lesson 1: Forms of Energy, we will continue identifying different forms of energy and exploring how energy is used in our everyday lives.

Reading, Writing & Social Studies

Reading

  • In Module 3, Week 3, we will read Why Is the Statue of Liberty Green? and practice identifying the central idea and supporting details, and determining the author’s purpose. Our vocabulary strategy will focus on synonyms and antonyms.

Writing

  • In Module 3, Week 3, we will continue practicing how to write a well-organized paragraph with a topic sentence, three supporting details, and a concluding sentence. We will also practice the cursive letters b and k.

Grammar

  • In Module 3, Week 3, we will learn about abstract nouns and practice identifying and using nouns that name ideas, feelings, and qualities.

Social Studies

  • In Chapter 2, Lesson 4, we will explore how people affect the environment and learn how human activities can change the world around us.
