Math & Science

Math

  • Read and write numbers to 10,000 in standard, expanded, and word form
  • Compose and decompose numbers using place value

Science

  • Explore the properties of matter
  • Compare materials and objects based on properties such as mass, hardness, and texture

Reading, Writing & Social Studies

Reading

  • Explore point of view
  • Make and confirm predictions
  • Identify literary elements and determine theme
  • Use context clues to determine the meaning of unfamiliar words

Writing

  • Begin writing personal narratives
  • Practice the cursive letters c and a

Grammar

  • Write simple sentences
  • Identify the subject and predicate in a sentence

Social Studies

  • Explore different types of communities, why communities are formed, and the characteristics they share
