Weekly Update, Families!

Hello Barracuda Families! Next week, students will take the Progress Monitoring I (PM1) FAST Reading Assessment on Wednesday. Please make sure your child gets plenty of sleep the night before, eats a healthy breakfast, and arrives at school on time so they are ready for testing.

Spelling Words

Focus: words with soft c, soft g, and the trigraphs tch and dge. Moving forward, students will take a Word Work Test instead of a traditional spelling test. Rather than memorizing a list of words, students will be assessed on their ability to understand and apply the weekly phonics/spelling skill to help them read and spell words. Please note: the example words below are not the exact words on the test — they show the type of words that apply to this week's skill, and you're welcome to use them to practice with your child at home.
